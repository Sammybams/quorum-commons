# Member Workflow Test Pass

Use this checklist after syncing at least one opportunity, one receipt/payment signal, and one task-like message from Community Inbox.

## Admin Flow

1. Sign in as a workspace admin.
2. Open `Members`.
3. Create an invite link for the `Member` role.
4. Confirm the pending/member sections render correctly.
5. Open `Community Inbox`.
6. Confirm at least one meaningful opportunity or receipt highlight is visible.
7. Open `Opportunities`.
8. Click `Refresh matches` on an extracted opportunity.
9. Confirm recommended matches appear.
10. Mark one match as `Contacted` and then `Assigned`.
11. Confirm the opportunity can move to `In progress`, `Filled`, or `Closed`.
12. Open `Community Inbox` and confirm a task-like message can be turned into a task with `Create task`.
13. Assign that task to a member or officer.
14. Open `Financial Health` and click `Refresh score`.
15. Confirm the trend line, partner-ready view, and evidence trail render.

## Member Flow

1. Open the invite link in a fresh browser profile or incognito window.
2. Complete signup and join the workspace.
3. Confirm the workspace role is `Member`.
4. Confirm the member nav is trimmed compared with admin.
5. Open `Opportunities`.
6. Confirm `Recommended for you` renders above the broader community board.
7. Click `I’m interested` on one opportunity.
8. Open the notifications dropdown and confirm recommendation/workflow notices can be seen.
9. Open `Tasks` and confirm any assigned task is visible.
10. Return to the admin account and confirm the member now appears as `Interested`.

## Receipt And Inbox Flow

1. In a synced group, send a receipt-like image or payment proof.
2. Wait for Community Inbox to refresh in the background.
3. Confirm the item appears as a receipt or contribution-related highlight.
4. Confirm the item shows one of these states clearly: `Matched`, `Needs review`, or `Unlinked`.
5. If the amount is meaningful, confirm admins receive an in-app notification.
6. Refresh `Financial Health` and confirm the receipt trail contributes to the profile and evidence list.

## Task Extraction Flow

1. In a synced group, send a message that contains a concrete action item.
2. Open `Community Inbox`.
3. Confirm the message is tagged as a task signal or appears in the review queue.
4. Use `Create task`.
5. Assign it to a member.
6. Confirm the assignee receives an in-app notification and can see it in `Tasks`.
